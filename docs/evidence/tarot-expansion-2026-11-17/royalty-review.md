# Historical ruler image review — 17 November 2026

The root reviewer inspected all 145 imported royalty/history images across four
contact sheets. This establishes visible-content and framing review, not proof
that a posthumous depiction records the subject's real appearance. The source
article/entity and file attribution are separate identity evidence.

Rejected or referred for replacement: text-only coins, calligraphic name discs,
a territorial map, an inscribed pillar, illegible images, and multi-person scenes
whose named subject is unclear. A ruler's coin can depict a deity or another
person: association with the ruler's article does not establish portrait identity.
The importer owner has exact IDs and applies the final exclusions/replacements.

Subject-focused crops were requested for selected full-length statues, equestrian
figures and paintings that make the face too small at the reading's portrait size.
Known historical depictions can remain when the subject is clearly identified
and visible; the UI does not label every source image a photograph.

The Lakshmibai source was checked separately: its file description identifies a
posthumous portrait, rather than a photograph taken during her life. The reference
is acceptable as a historical depiction with its original source credit.
[Source file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rani_of_jhansi.jpg).

The first ten fictional additions were also inspected. Baba Yaga, Sun Wukong and
Winnie-the-Pooh were referred for replacement/exclusion because fine lines, low
contrast or a very small subject do not make a readable phone portrait.
All original 48 accepted identities and artworks remain preserved.

Final accepted counts and the disposition of each flagged new asset belong to
references.md and the versioned manifest; intermediate acquisition totals are not
release acceptance. Contact sheets are retained in task-owned ignored evidence.
