# Historical figure image recovery

Reviewed 17 November 2026. Fourteen sourced images were repaired and accepted after inspecting the original image, the final 320 × 400 asset and an 88 × 110 card-size contact sheet. Their exact source, crop, dimensions, attribution, rights metadata and hashes are preserved in [the review receipt](reference-review/ruler-cards-recovery.json). The final assets total 232,110 bytes. Canonical identities and reading context were unchanged.

The review deliberately rejected an attractive but incorrectly associated Suppiluliuma statue: its subject is a later ruler of Patin, not the Hittite king Suppiluliuma I. Generic Vikramaditya artwork was also not substituted for Chandragupta II. Suppiluliuma I, Chandragupta II, Samudragupta and Ajatashatru were handed to the art owner for separately reviewed, clearly labelled original illustrations. Those four are outside this receipt.

Useful source checks include [Greer’s Hooke reconstruction and provenance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:13_Portrait_of_Robert_Hooke.JPG), the [Academy of Korean Studies Gwanggaeto portrait record](https://dh.aks.ac.kr/Encyves/wiki/index.php/%EA%B4%91%EA%B0%9C%ED%86%A0%EB%8C%80%EC%99%95_%ED%91%9C%EC%A4%80%EC%98%81%EC%A0%95), and the [Patin statue’s explicit identification](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Antakya_Archaeological_Museum_Statue_of_Suppiluliuma_sept_2019_5789.jpg).

Limits: several early figures have later, stylized or imagined depictions. Charles’s small manuscript source remains visibly soft when enlarged; no artificial facial detail was added. Full calligraphy uses containment rather than cutting letters to fill the frame. These three nonfigurative assets have exact artwork titles and source links so the product shows its “Artwork reference” caption. This establishes image identity, legibility and recorded provenance; it does not establish authenticated historical likenesses or universal reading quality.

Cleanup: source downloads, original before-copies and review sheets are retained in the ignored `output/tarot-reference-import-20261117/ruler-cards-sources` folder pending the final merged-asset hash check. No application rooms, browser contexts, external records or local services were created or closed for this image batch. No reading-provider calls were made.
