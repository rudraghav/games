## Explicit artwork references

For artists whose lead images show their work, the app uses an explicit `artwork` image type with a reviewed work title and attribution URL, instead of presenting the work as a likeness. The reading labels it **Artwork reference**, and the credit names the exact work. Art and meme compositions receive more space on desktop and sit above the connection on small phones. Generated illustrations receive a separate **Original illustration** caption.

Fourteen existing sources were resolved without replacing their pixels: thirteen are correctly attributed works and Phidias is a named historical sculptural portrait. Three unsuitable images were replaced with named works: Lysippos’s inscribed base with a Roman Apoxyomenos copy; an unidentified female head for Scopas with a Pothos copy; and Ahmad Lahori’s seal with the Taj Mahal, described as associated architecture rather than a portrait. Full 320px replacements and all seventeen final88px assets were inspected. The attribution URL preserves copy/association qualifications. Banksy’s underlying artwork remains copyrighted despite separate photographer licence terms; the manifest does not label the whole work openly licensed.
