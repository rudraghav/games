## Visual and identity checks

All24 source scenes/templates and their final files were inspected at 320px, desktop 176px and phone 240px widths. The output is 22 landscape320×240 images and 2 portrait320×400 images, totalling 260,912 bytes. No generated or reconstructed frames were used. Two crops were revised to make Baburao and Padmanabha Simha clearer.

The complete meaningful composition is retained: both Spider-Man figures, both Two Buttons panels, the first two This Is Fine panels, and Majnu’s entire painting. Low-resolution texture remains in Surprised Pikachu, Michael Scott, Khan Dada and some circulated film frames; no face enhancement was used. Private disposable review sheets are under `output/tarot-reference-import-20261117/meme-sources/final-{320,176,240}-{1,2,3}.png`.

Corrected source mistakes include the unnamed machine-class professor in 3 Idiots (Achyut Potdar, neither Virus nor the librarian), Padmasri in Dookudu versus Padmanabha Simha in Baadshah, and a mislabelled Kill Bill Pandey television GIF replaced by the military-uniform entrance in Race Gurram. A back-facing Baburao candidate was replaced with the clearer no-risk reaction.

Scene identity remains separate from actor identity. The two Raju variants share one character family. Michael Scott and Peter Parker scenes share the corresponding existing character families; the six Brahmanandam roles remain distinct. No Wikidata IDs were invented for meme variants. Selection must be situationally relevant, allow at most one meme, and avoid treating a reaction as evidence of an actor’s private personality.

## Scope and cleanup

This asset work created no production rooms, browser contexts or AI reading calls. Downloaded sources, crop/frame receipts and review sheets remain ignored until the merged manifest is verified. Only the 24 new `meme-` staged assets were written. Source inspection does not establish that every future reading will use a meme accurately; the frozen-catalogue matrix and saved-room checks must evaluate that separately.
