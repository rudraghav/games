# Historical-reference image review

Root visually inspected all 189 new royalty and 240 politics portraits, alongside
canonical identities and source metadata. Initial review accepted 340 and held
89 for correction: 65 framing issues and 24 source/identity issues. These are
new drafts, separate from the earlier 1,342-reference baseline.

All 65 framing corrections use pixels from the original credited source. The
reviewer inspected original-image previews, chose individual crop bounds, then
checked every before/after at the reading's 88×110 size. Resulting assets remain
320×400 WebPs; byte counts and SHA-256 values match their accepted patches.
Source backups and exact crop rectangles are retained through integration.

Coins, reliefs, traditional paintings and statues are attributed historical
depictions, not photographs or guarantees of an authentic surviving likeness.
The [Eannatum portrait extraction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eannatum_portrait_2500-2400_BCE.jpg)
helps identify the ruler in the original Stele of Vultures fragment. Umar's
calligraphy is an intentional non-figural representation; the review does not
replace it with a purportedly authentic face.

The 24 source repairs include buildings, maps, cartouches, ambiguous group scenes
and unclear statues. They remain pending until a verified depiction or a clearly
labelled original illustration is reviewed. No good name is removed solely
because its first image is unsuitable. Source review is not a claim of independent
facial recognition for every historical figure.
