Evidence notation:

- **S** — current source, module rules/README, renderer, projection and relevant test assertions reviewed. This does not mean every branch was executed.
- **R** — current local browser renders of legally reached engine projections are available in this directory's capture manifest. They are design-harness evidence, not persisted multiplayer matches. Representative images were reviewed; a phase's existence in the source does not establish a browser interaction pass for that phase.
- **P** — fresh pure-engine legal trajectory or explicitly labelled controlled probe, identified in the row. Legal trajectories begin at setup; controlled states are not represented as naturally reached.
- **A** — current assets inspected, with the inventory in the linked subreport.
- **E** — independent rules/reference comparison, with authority and any limitation in the linked subreport. Original house experiences use their own documented rules as authority.

**Saved-match lifecycle status is intentionally not aggregated here.** The parent audit owns the final verification receipt for actual API/persistence, phone/desktop completion, saved results, profile/history, refresh, rematch and switching games with the same group. Those runs were still being reconciled when this table was assembled. Do not infer universal completion from the current renders, older coverage counts, available actions or passing pure-engine tests. A remaining branch below means that branch is not established by this consolidated evidence, even if the ordinary lifecycle is separately verified.
