# Independent review: modern, cooperative, word, resource, board and party games

Review date: 2 November 2026. Scope: 16 registered experiences in the current local checkout. Application code, settings, servers and production data were not changed by this review. This report is one part of the parent audit; its browser, transport and full-suite evidence must be read alongside this report.

## Verdict and evidence boundaries

The inspected games have substantial, game-specific implementations. Their core decisions are not generic card actions pasted over incompatible rules. Particularly useful details already exist: Trio's fixed memory positions, Azul's placement/overflow previews, The Gang's final-chip warning, 6 nimmt!'s hidden simultaneous submissions, Sea Salt & Paper's actor-only draw and discard inspection, boop.'s alternate win, explicit checkers jump continuation, and optional participation in every party mode. Preserve those choices.

The strongest current problems in this subset are missing explanations at consequential decisions and results. Gem Market does not preview its automatic payment. Sea Salt & Paper hides effects in compact choices and does not explain the zero-stock consequence where Stop/Last Chance/Finish are offered. The Gang retains a false privacy label after reveal and does not identify the comparison that caused an alarm. Board results identify the winner without explaining the decisive line or draw condition. These are implementation-ready improvements, not reasons for a new UI framework.

No new unequivocal wrong-winner or concealed-card leak is established in these 16 engines. That is a bounded finding, not a safety guarantee. Sea Salt & Paper's stock/Last Chance interaction is a confirmed behavioral discrepancy with the licensed BGA implementation, but its local README explicitly documents its behavior and the printed publisher wording is broad. It requires an edition decision before being called a rules defect.

Evidence labels used below:

- **Source:** current engine, projection, renderer, README and named tests inspected. A test's existence is not reported as fresh execution.
- **Legal probe:** fresh deterministic transitions from `setup`, selecting only enumerated legal actions, without replacing engine state.
- **Browser image:** current local rendered legal projection captured by the parent audit, not a saved multiplayer match or production evidence.
- **Independent rules:** primary publisher/federation source retrieved in this review; licensed implementation is distinguished from a rulebook.
- **Unchecked:** not established by this sub-review, even where historical evidence or a related test exists.

The fresh probe script and results are `modern-board-party-probes.py` and `modern-board-party-probes.json` in this directory. It records public summaries and legal-choice ordinals, not hidden deck order or unrevealed identities. Parent screenshots inspected directly here: `gem-market-390.jpg`, `the-gang-390.jpg`, `sea-salt-paper-320.jpg`, and `boop-390.jpg`. Their QA controls are not production app chrome and must not be used to infer production first-fold space.

## Findings and implementation acceptance

### MBP-01 — Sea Salt & Paper: an important stock consequence is missing from the decision

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed missing decision information; related P1 investigation before changing the scoring convention.

**State and observation:** In a legally reached two-player seed-0 round, after 83 legal actions the active player has 12 card points and stock is empty. Finish gives both players zero; Stop gives 10/12. Last Chance remains available, announces that everyone else has a final turn, permits a discard draw, then the first final turn's Finish gives everyone zero. A boat can also lead to another discard draw; Stop then gives 10/13. Four-player seed 45 reproduces the boundary and the first final finisher closes the round before the remaining two players take their advertised final turns. Starting Last Chance with two stock cards and exhausting them during the first final turn also gives zero (two-player seed 0; four-player seed 117).

**Evidence / cause:** `backend/app/games/sea_salt_paper/engine.py:124` checks empty deck before the caller/final queue; `:169` permits a boat draw from discard; `:191` resolves Stop immediately; `:193` starts Last Chance without checking stock. The current README explicitly documents final-turn exhaustion voiding the round. `apps/web/src/games/sea/PaperTable.tsx:38` shows stock only in the draw phase, while `:156` renders the later end-turn choices using generic action labels. The action labels at engine `:94` do not explain the different zero-stock outcomes. All 12 branch probes preserve the 58-card inventory and the input state.

**Independent authority:** [Bombyx's current English rulebook, PDF page 1, “End of the Round”](https://studiobombyx.com/assets/SSAP_rulebook_EN-3.pdf) says: “If the deck is empty at the end of a player’s turn, the round ends immediately without scoring.” Its Last Chance paragraph separately grants opponents final turns. In contrast, the licensed edition developer's pinned [NextPlayer.php, source lines 32–52](https://github.com/thoun/seasaltpaper/blob/b17c1b0c7f5ec55c8c5bce573501e8af6f68141f/modules/php/States/NextPlayer.php#L32) only applies empty-deck cancellation when no ending has been announced; announced Last Chance completes and scores. Its [PlayCards.php, lines 163–169 and 387–402](https://github.com/thoun/seasaltpaper/blob/b17c1b0c7f5ec55c8c5bce573501e8af6f68141f/modules/php/States/PlayCards.php#L163) permits boat continuation with discards and sets an announcement before end-turn resolution. This is a useful licensed reference, not a publisher erratum. No definitive erratum was established.

**Impact:** A player can choose Last Chance expecting a scored bet and instead cancel the round. The consequence is especially hard to infer because the stock count disappears at that choice.

**Proposed change / owner:** Resolve the intended edition in `sea_salt_paper/README.md` and the engine's round-closing transition contract. Then project the relevant public ending consequence; `PaperTable` should show it beside Stop/Last Chance/Finish, without a second modal. If retaining the existing convention, explicitly say that ending this turn scores zero and that an uncompleted Last Chance may be cancelled. If adopting the licensed reference's behavior, a declared Last Chance must bypass ordinary exhaustion cancellation. Do not remove boat/discard continuation merely because stock is empty: the reference supports it.

**Acceptance:** Replay all 12 recorded branches at two/four seats. Assert exact points, recipients of final turns, protected hands, next starter, conservation and no mutation; add stock-one/fish-exhaustion and four-mermaid override combinations. At 320/390px, the actor can distinguish Stop, Last Chance and Finish consequences before submission. A refresh at the branch retains the same explanation. Do not call a new convention verified until documentation and independent authority agree.

### MBP-02 — Gem Market: selecting a purchase does not reveal the actual payment

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed source-level information omission; no payment arithmetic bug established.

**Evidence:** `backend/app/games/gem_market/engine.py:27` determines discounted payment and spends matching gems before gold. Legal buy labels at `:76` contain the name and prestige; projection `:157` supplies costs, wallet and discounts, but no payment/shortfall breakdown. `apps/web/src/games/gems/GemsTable.tsx:74` sends only the selected name and ordinary actions to `ActionPanel`. `apps/web/src/games/shared/ActionPanel.tsx:176` reduces an unavailable card to “This card has no available move.”

**Impact / cause:** The player must mentally subtract five discounts, compare five token balances and infer automatic gold spending. An unaffordable card does not identify the missing resources; an unavailable reservation does not explain the three-card limit. The engine already has the authoritative calculation, but the decision contract discards its explanation.

**Proposed change / owner:** Add a viewer-authorized purchase preview derived from the engine's payment function: printed cost, applied discounts, exact colored/gold tokens spent and post-purchase wallet. Supply concise unavailability reasons for purchase/reservation. Map through generated wire types and the API-client boundary; extend `GemsTable`/the existing `ActionPanel` selection area. Retain the original Gem Market inventory and documented automatic-payment rule.

**Acceptance:** For a legal market purchase and own reserved purchase, show exact payment including gold and zero-cost purchases; submit and compare actual wallet delta. For unaffordable cards, show the exact shortfall without suggesting hidden tier contents. For three reserved cards, explain why Reserve is unavailable. Preview, Clear and changing tier leave the market/hand position stable on 320/390/768/1440px; server remains authoritative on stale submission.

### MBP-03 — The Gang: the privacy label remains false after reveal

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed misleading privacy copy, not an unauthorized leak.

**Evidence:** `apps/web/src/games/coop-number/GangTable.tsx:79` always labels the viewer's pocket as private and `:83` always says only the viewer can see it. Engine `backend/app/games/the_gang/engine.py:134` legitimately exposes each revealed hand to everyone. The parent's current six-player heist-review screenshot `screenshots/the-gang-390.jpg` visibly contains both that private claim and the same public cards below.

**Impact / cause:** The UI contradicts the rules and the actual projection at showdown/review. Privacy status is hardcoded instead of following `detail.revealed`.

**Proposed change / owner:** In `GangTable`, use the revealed list to label both visible copy and the section's accessible name. Keep the private wording before that player's reveal; use “Revealed to the crew” afterward. Consider avoiding the duplicate viewer cards at review while retaining the shared ordered comparison.

**Acceptance:** Inspect the viewer before reveal, after their reveal while others wait, after complete reveal, and after reconnect into heist review. Labels match each state and another player's cards stay concealed until authorized reveal.

### MBP-04 — The Gang: an alarm does not explain which ranking was wrong

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed outcome-information omission.

**Evidence:** `_settle` compares full poker ranks at `backend/app/games/the_gang/engine.py:87`, then stores only success and a generic message. Projection `:139` exposes category names but no best-five comparison/kicker explanation. `GangTable.tsx:187` shows generic alarm copy; `:204` renders hands/category names. The current 390px screenshot has several “Pair” hands and an alarm, requiring players to reconstruct the ranking themselves.

**Impact / cause:** New players cannot readily learn why two same-category hands were ordered incorrectly. Correct engine logic is compressed into a boolean and category labels.

**Proposed change / owner:** Preserve a public showdown explanation from the common poker evaluator: chip order, actual rank/tie groups, first inversion and the decisive rank/kicker. Expose only after the relevant cards are public. `GangTable` should highlight the first failing adjacent pair and permit inspecting the best five.

**Acceptance:** Same pair with different kickers, board-playing exact ties, different categories and total team loss each show a correct plain explanation. No unrevealed opponent rank or best-five IDs appear during chip estimation. A six-seat phone result does not require comparing two distant horizontal rails.

### MBP-05 — Sea Salt & Paper: compact choices hide the card rule at the decision

**Priority / classification:** P2 source-confirmed information/accessibility gap; actual touch and assistive-technology interaction remains to be measured.

**Evidence:** `packages/ui/src/components/PaperPlayingCard.tsx:29` omits the subtitle when compact. `apps/web/src/games/sea/PaperTable.tsx:60` uses compact discard tops; `:87` uses compact private stock and crab choices. The normal hand later displays effect text, but those cards have already been selected. A native `title` is the only visible pointer fallback. The discard button's custom label at `:63` also replaces the default accessible label that includes the subtitle.

**Impact / cause:** A new touch player cannot inspect the duo effect or collector/multiplier rule while deciding which offered card to keep/take. Screen readers receive the effect for private choices, but not in the custom discard label. Compact presentation has also become an information policy.

**Proposed change / owner:** Reuse a selected-card detail area in the existing decision dock. In draft choices, selection displays family, color and rule before Keep. Provide a separately reachable inspect affordance for a public discard/crab card so inspection does not commit the draw. Include effect in accessible descriptions. Keep opponents' discard interiors hidden except to the crab actor.

**Acceptance:** All 14 families are identifiable with effect/scoring text by touch, keyboard and pointer before commitment. A screen reader can distinguish discard number, card identity, color and effect. Cancel/clear returns to the same choice without drawing or revealing additional cards. No hover dependency or deck/private-hand exposure.

### MBP-06 — Four in a Row, checkers and boop.: results lack decisive board metadata

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed outcome-information omission; Four has a fresh legal proof.

**Evidence:** `backend/app/games/board/four.py:44` detects a line but stores only winner/message; `board/view.py:15` projects only dimensions, draw and last move. Legal columns `[0,1,0,1,0,1,0]` end with a vertical win and detail `{rows:6, columns:7, draw:false, last_move:14}`. `BoardTable.tsx:97` highlights last move only, and `:112` uses generic completion/draw copy. Checkers `backend/app/games/board/checkers.py:98` has no-move victory and `:103` merges quiet-80/repetition-three draws into one message; `:121` omits the reason. boop. `backend/app/games/boop/engine.py:123` has three-cat/eight-cat victory but its projection does not explain which condition won.

**Impact / cause:** Players see that a match ended without a clear explanation. The engines discard the decisive cells/reason when producing the outcome; the renderer cannot reliably reconstruct why.

**Proposed change / owner:** Add a small typed public outcome contract, with game-specific reason and relevant cells. Board UI highlights the four or three winning cells; checkers says no legal move, third repetition, or 40 moves per side without capture/man move. Eight-cat boop. explains that alternative. Reversi/Mancala retain their visible final counts rather than adopting a line highlight.

**Acceptance:** Both diagonal Four wins, horizontal/vertical, board-full draw; checkers elimination/blocked opponent/both draws; boop. three-cat/eight-cat all show the right explanation after refresh. Highlights never imply that a pushed opponent line immediately wins. Reduced motion still shows the result. This does not add forced confirmation to ordinary board moves.

### MBP-07 — Gem Market: phase-specific instructions contradict forced actions

**Priority / classification:** P2 confirmed current browser/source issue.

**Evidence:** `GemsTable.tsx:83` says to select a development to buy/reserve whenever none is selected. In the current legal four-seat `return_tokens` screenshot `screenshots/gem-market-390.jpg`, this appears directly over Return buttons. The engine's accurate message at `gem_market/engine.py:104` is not used there. The same instruction is also inappropriate for the patron phase.

**Impact / cause:** Players are directed toward an unavailable normal-turn action during a mandatory cleanup decision. The override is keyed only to selection, not phase.

**Proposed change / owner:** Use phase-specific guidance in `GemsTable` and retain generic `ActionPanel` rendering. State the number of gems that must be returned; highlight the returnable wallet resources. In patron phase, ask for one eligible patron and show qualifying discounts. Normal market selection copy remains for `playing`.

**Acceptance:** Return from 11/12/13 to ten updates the remaining amount after every response, survives refresh, and never offers purchase guidance. Multiple eligible patrons permit exactly one choice. At 320px the required action and explanation are visible together; selection-clearing does not erase the phase instruction.

### MBP-08 — boop.: the graduation preview is not a touch-first interaction

**Priority / classification:** P2 usability improvement, source-confirmed interaction asymmetry; not a rules bug or demonstrated inability to play.

**Evidence:** `apps/web/src/games/boop/BoopTable.tsx:98` highlights a graduation on hover/focus and commits immediately on click. There is no select/confirm state for touch. Coordinate labels do provide an alternative way to identify the three cells. The current 390px screenshot shows a graduation panel with its options below the visible panel top; the parent's broader dock/scroll review should establish any actual obstruction.

**Impact / cause:** Overlapping lines are much easier to compare with a pointer/keyboard preview than with a tap that commits. The consequential three-piece removal uses a hover affordance for inspection.

**Proposed change / owner:** In graduation only, tap selects a line and keeps its three cells highlighted; a visible confirmation names the cells and cat returns, with Clear to change it. Preserve direct square placement and the explicit eight-piece single return; do not add dialogs to every move.

**Acceptance:** Legally reach overlapping lines and the eight-piece alternative. Touch/keyboard/pointer can preview either line, change it and commit exactly one; the board position remains stable and the selected line plus confirm fit without losing context. Refresh clears only unsubmitted local selection.

### MBP-09 — Gem Market: the displayed tier count is hardcoded

**Priority / classification:** P3 source-confirmed late-game inconsistency; depletion state not freshly reached here.

`GemsTable.tsx:199` always displays four cards, while `gem_market/engine.py:110` removes a bought/reserved card without replacement after that tier is exhausted. Derive the visible count from that tier's projected cards. Acceptance: a depleted tier with three, one and zero cards labels the actual count; no private stock identities are exposed. Fold this small fix into MBP-02/07.
